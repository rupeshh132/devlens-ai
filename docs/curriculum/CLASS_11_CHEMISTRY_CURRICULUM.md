## Formative Enrichment Modules (CASE 2027)

> [!NOTE]
> Under the **Common Annual School Examination (CASE) 2027** guidelines, two core chemical domains are designated for formative assessment to reduce examination anxiety while preserving conceptual continuity for higher secondary chemistry and national competitive examinations (JEE Main / NEET UG):
> 1. **s-Block and p-Block Elements:** Electronic configuration, atomic/ionic radii, ionization enthalpy, hydration enthalpy, periodic trends, and anomalous behavior of the first element in each group.
> 2. **The Gaseous State:** Qualitative treatment of gas laws, ideal gas equation ($PV = nRT$), kinetic theory, real gases, and deviations from ideality (Van der Waals equation).
> In our learning management platform, these are structured as **Chapter 10** (`s-Block and p-Block Elements`) and **Chapter 11** (`The Gaseous State: States of Matter`).

## Practicals Detailed Syllabus (Core Experiments, Salt Analysis & Volumetric Estimation)

### Part A: Basic Laboratory Techniques
1. Cutting a glass tube and glass rod.
2. Bending a glass tube.
3. Drawing out a glass jet.
4. Boring a cork.

### Part B: Characterization and Purification of Chemical Substances
1. Determination of melting point of an organic compound.
2. Determination of boiling point of an organic compound.
3. Crystallization of an impure sample of: Alum, Copper Sulphate, or Benzoic Acid.

### Part C: Quantitative Estimation (Volumetric Analysis - 8 Marks)
1. Using mechanical / electronic balance.
2. Preparation of standard solution of Oxalic Acid ($0.1\,M$).
3. Determination of strength of a given Sodium Hydroxide ($NaOH$) solution by titrating against standard Oxalic Acid solution.
4. Preparation of standard solution of Sodium Carbonate ($Na_2CO_3$, $0.1\,M$).
5. Determination of strength of a given Hydrochloric Acid ($HCl$) solution by titrating against standard Sodium Carbonate solution.

### Part D: Content-Based Experiments (6 Marks)
1. **Experiments based on pH:**
   - Determination of pH of fruit juices, acids, bases, and salts using pH paper or universal indicator.
   - Comparing pH of solutions of strong and weak acids of same concentration.
   - Study pH change in the titration of a strong base using universal indicator.
   - Study pH change by common-ion effect in weak acids and bases.
2. **Chemical Equilibrium:**
   - Shift in equilibrium between ferric ions and thiocyanate ions:
     $$\text{Fe}^{3+} (\text{yellow}) + \text{SCN}^- \rightleftharpoons [\text{Fe(SCN)}]^{2+} (\text{deep red})$$
   - Shift in equilibrium between $[\text{Co(H}_2\text{O)}_6]^{2+}$ (pink) and chloride ions:
     $$[\text{Co(H}_2\text{O)}_6]^{2+} + 4\text{Cl}^- \rightleftharpoons [\text{CoCl}_4]^{2-} (\text{blue}) + 6\text{H}_2\text{O}$$
