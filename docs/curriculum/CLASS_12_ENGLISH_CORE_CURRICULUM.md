# Class 12 English Core (Subject Code: 301) Curriculum Architecture

## Overview & Administrative Specifications
- **Board / Authority:** Directorate of Education, GNCT Delhi & CBSE Senior Secondary Curriculum (Session 2026–27)
- **Exam Type ID:** `e334760b-a4d0-4843-9234-a0ab75684ff3` (Class 12)
- **Subject ID:** `65d29b56-0301-0000-0000-000000000012`
- **Subject Code:** `301`
- **Subject Name:** `English Core`
- **Order Index:** `5`
- **Prescribed Textbooks:**
  1. *Flamingo: English Reader*, NCERT for Class XII (Core Prose & Poetry)
  2. *Vistas: Supplementary Reader*, NCERT for Class XII
  3. CBSE Curriculum Guidelines for Reading, Writing Skills & ASL
- **Distribution of Marks (Theory Examination: 80 Marks, Time: 3 Hours):**
  - **Section A: Reading Skills (22 Marks)**
    - Unseen Passage (Discursive / Factual / Literary) — 12 Marks
    - Unseen Case-Based Factual Passage (with statistical data, charts, graphs) — 10 Marks
  - **Section B: Creative Writing Skills (18 Marks)**
    - Short Composition: Notice Writing (up to 50 words) — 4 Marks
    - Short Composition: Formal & Informal Invitations and Replies (up to 50 words) — 4 Marks
    - Long Composition: Letter Writing (Job Application with Bio-Data / Letter to Editor, 120-150 words) — 5 Marks
    - Long Composition: Article Writing OR Report Writing (120-150 words) — 5 Marks
  - **Section C: Literature Text Book and Supplementary Text (40 Marks)**
    - Flamingo Prose (8 Chapters) + Flamingo Poetry (5 Poems)
    - Vistas Supplementary Reader (6 Chapters)
  - **Internal Assessment: ALS & Project Work (20 Marks)**
    - Assessment of Listening Skills (ALS) — 5 Marks
    - Assessment of Speaking Skills (ASL) — 5 Marks
    - Project Work & Viva Voce — 10 Marks
  - **Theory Examination Total:** 80 Marks
  - **Internal Assessment Total:** 20 Marks
  - **Grand Total:** 100 Marks

> [!NOTE]
> Per the official Directorate of Education Delhi & CBSE 2026-27 circular, the rationalized syllabus explicitly omits deleted chapters ("An Elementary School Classroom in a Slum" in Flamingo Poetry, and "Should Wizard Hit Mommy?" & "Evans Tries an O-level" in Vistas). The 27 core evaluative chapters constitute 100% of the summative and formative board curriculum.

The curriculum features high-impact one-shot masterclasses delivered primarily by India's celebrated CBSE English educator **Simran Sahni**, along with CBSE board official assessment guidelines.

Every chapter is structured into contiguous, verified conceptual subtopics (82 topics total across the subject) with an exact, **100% zero-gap continuous timeline** starting strictly at `0s`. All outro promotional screens have been cleanly trimmed before video end cards.
