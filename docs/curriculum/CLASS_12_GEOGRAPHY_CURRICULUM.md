## Pedagogical Continuity & Masterclasses
The curriculum features high-impact one-shot masterclasses delivered by India's preeminent CBSE Humanities educators:
- **Part A & Part B (Chapters 1 to 17):** **The Twin Bros - Humanities Ka Ghar** (Class 12 Rapid Revision Series) — renowned for NCERT line-by-line concept breakdown, diagrammatic analysis, case studies, and board exam patterns.
- **Part C: Practical Work (Chapters 18 to 20):** **AC eduhub** (NCERT Class 12 Geography Practical Series) — comprehensive numerical step-by-step calculations for mean, median, mode, standard deviation, and graphic construction.
- **Part C: Practical Work (Chapter 21):** **Adda247 Humanities Class 12** — complete conceptual breakdown of GIS, spatial data models, raster vs vector formats, and overlay analysis.
- **Board Map Work (Chapter 22):** **Humanities Wallah (PW)** — complete 10-marks Board map work covering all prescribed World and India political maps.

Each chapter is divided into contiguous, verified conceptual topics (88 topics total across the subject) with an exact, **100% zero-gap continuous timeline** starting strictly at `0s`.
