## Formative Enrichment Module (CASE 2027)

> [!NOTE]
> Under the **Common Annual School Examination (CASE) 2027** guidelines, **Digestion and Absorption** is designated for formative assessment to reduce academic burden while maintaining complete conceptual grounding for medical entrance (NEET UG) and foundational physiology:
> - **Alimentary canal & digestive glands**, role of digestive enzymes and gastrointestinal hormones.
> - **Peristalsis, digestion, absorption and assimilation** of proteins, carbohydrates, and fats.
> - **Calorific values** of macronutrients; egestion.
> - **Nutritional and digestive disorders:** PEM (Kwashiorkor, Marasmus), indigestion, constipation, vomiting, jaundice, diarrhea.
> In our learning management platform, this is encapsulated in **Chapter 20: Digestion and Absorption (Formative & Bridge Module)** (`65d29b56-0440-1100-0000-000000000020`).

## Practicals Detailed Syllabus (Core Experiments & Spotting)

### Part A: List of Laboratory Experiments
1. **Floral Dissection:** Study and description of locally available flowering plants from family Solanaceae (dissection of whorls, anther and ovary chamber counts, floral formula, floral diagram, root, stem, leaf characteristics).
2. **Plant Anatomy Slides:** Preparation and study of T.S. of dicot and monocot roots and stems (primary).
3. **Osmosis:** Study of osmosis by potato osmometer.
4. **Plasmolysis:** Study of plasmolysis in epidermal peels (e.g., *Rhoeo* / lily leaves or scale leaves of onion).
5. **Stomatal Distribution:** Distribution of stomata on upper and lower surfaces of leaves.
6. **Transpiration:** Comparative study of rates of transpiration on upper and lower surfaces of leaves.
7. **Biochemical Food Tests:** Test for presence of sugar, starch, proteins, and fats in plant and animal materials.
8. **Pigment Chromatography:** Separation of plant pigments through paper chromatography.
9. **Plant Respiration:** Rate of respiration in flower buds/leaf tissue and germinating seeds.
10. **Clinical Urine Tests:**
    - Test for presence of **urea** in urine.
    - Test for presence of **sugar** (glucose) in urine.
    - Test for presence of **albumin** in urine.
    - Test for presence of **bile salts** in urine.

### Part B: Spotting & Identification (Models, Slides, Virtual Specimens)
1. **Microscopy:** Parts and mechanical operation of a compound microscope.
2. **Identification of Cryptogams & Phanerogams:** Bacteria, *Oscillatoria*, *Spirogyra*, *Rhizopus*, mushroom, yeast, liverwort, moss, fern, pine, monocot plant, dicot plant, and lichen.
3. **Virtual Specimen Identification:** *Amoeba*, *Hydra*, Liver fluke, *Ascaris*, Leech, Earthworm, Prawn, Silkworm, Honey bee, Snail, Starfish, Shark, Rohu, Frog, Lizard, Pigeon, and Rabbit.
4. **Cell Division Slides:** Mitosis in onion root tip cells and animal cells (grasshopper) from permanent slides.
5. **Inflorescence Types:** Cymose and Racemose inflorescences.
6. **Human Osteology:** Human skeleton and different types of joints (ball & socket, hinge, pivot, gliding, saddle) using virtual models.
